My Founder Story – The Journey of NOIR ROAST

NOIR ROAST started with two things I have always loved — chocolate and coffee. Somewhere along the way, my growing passion for coffee sparked an unusual thought: why should we only drink coffee when we could actually eat it? That little thought became the beginning of something special. I wanted to create something different, something that would surprise people and offer a completely new way to experience coffee.

After exploring possibilities and experimenting with flavours, I found my inspiration in the rare peaberry coffee bean. I imagined combining its bold, intense coffee character with the richness of dark chocolate. There were ideas to test, details to perfect and plenty of learning along the way. From understanding the bean and getting the roast right to developing the chocolate coating, working on the packaging and building the brand from scratch, I was personally involved in bringing every part of this idea to life. It wasn’t simply about launching another chocolate product; it was about creating something I genuinely believed people would love.

That’s how NOIR ROAST was born — dark-roasted peaberry coffee beans wrapped in rich dark chocolate, bringing together two beautiful flavours in one unexpected bite.

Building this under Aureim has been a journey of self-belief, persistence and learning by doing. NOIR ROAST is more than a product for me; it is a reminder that sometimes the most interesting businesses begin with a simple thought and the courage to pursue it.
